## Method extracted

The speaker's content-ideation method:

1. Write ten things you can prove about your niche.
2. For each, ask why it matters beyond the expert definition.
3. Find the ordinary situation in which a non-expert experiences the issue.
4. Restate the fact through that situation without discarding the fact.
5. Repeat weekly to create useful, comprehensible content.

## Adaptation made here

The video teaches content ideation. This skill generalizes the method for positioning and white papers by adding:

- Explicit reader intention and belief shift
- Evidence classes and invalidation conditions
- Mechanism, situation, stakes, and action fields
- Claim fidelity and ethical-influence gates
- Ranking for argument selection

These additions prevent a useful simplification method from becoming unsupported contrarian copy.
