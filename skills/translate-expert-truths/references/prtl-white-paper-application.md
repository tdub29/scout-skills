# PRTL white-paper application

## Placement in the workflow

Run `translate-expert-truths` after the positioning audit has produced classified, cited truths. Run it before Minto structuring or prose drafting.

Use the complete research and skill inventory in `references/research-archive/2026-07-21-prtl-white-paper-positioning-inputs.md`.

## Working intention to verify

- **Reader:** Pre-seed or seed investor evaluating a software-led consumer-hardware company.
- **Action:** Advance diligence and fund the proof stage. Do not invent raise terms or check size.
- **Current belief:** PRTL may be another crowded, pre-launch AI photo frame with commodity hardware risk.
- **Required belief:** PRTL may own a hardware-flexible software layer for ambient home displays, with a capital-efficient next proof.
- **Tension:** Commodity hardware gamble versus a defensible coordination layer for the home.

Treat this as a hypothesis. Revise it when Trevor or stronger evidence says otherwise.

## Candidate truth domains to test

Do not accept these as facts. Verify each against current code and canonical strategy documents:

1. Phone as control plane; displays as ambient output.
2. Modes as the core product unit.
3. System-generated intent and context versus manual prompting.
4. Live data rendered as decor.
5. Multi-room visual coordination.
6. PRTL ships finished hardware while software is the moat.
7. Hardware flexibility and supplier strategy.
8. Emotional ambient-home value versus functional dashboard utility.
9. Category creation versus comparison with frames, art TVs, and smart displays.
10. Capital-efficient software-first proof before inventory exposure.

Use `.claude/rules/repo-reality.md` when product docs conflict with code. Use `Company/BEST-OFFERING-AND-ROADMAP-2026-07.md` and `Company/Product-Management/SUPPLIER-STATUS-2026-07.md` for current commercial strategy. Separate current capability, near-term plan, and long-term architecture.

## Required output addition

After `Fundamental Truths`, add:

### Reader-Stakes Translation

For each verified truth, provide:

| Truth | Investor situation/question | Stakes | Required belief shift | Shortest accurate claim | Evidence needed | Overclaim risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then name:

- Three truths that should govern the paper
- Three technically true points that readers will not care about without translation
- Three attractive claims blocked by missing evidence
- One governing thought the evidence can currently support

## PRTL-specific gates

- Do not collapse PRTL into "AI photo frame" unless describing the reader's current comparison set.
- Do not claim a home-display OS merely because the phrase is strategically desirable. Prove OS-level coordination and state what is current versus target.
- Do not imply multi-room orchestration, scheduling, or agentic intent is working end to end unless code proves it.
- Do not use competitor activity as customer demand proof.
- Do not present supplier quotes, pricing targets, margin models, or forecasts as validated economics.
- Do not make emotional language carry a technical claim that evidence cannot.

## Skill order

1. `deep-research` + repo inspection: establish evidence.
2. `grill-for-unknowns`: surface contradictions and missing decisions.
3. `product-marketing`, `customer-research`, `competitor-profiling`, `competitors`: build positioning inputs.
4. `translate-expert-truths`: connect verified truths to investor situations and stakes.
5. `marketing-psychology`, `behavioral-economics`, `behavior-design`, `persuasion-copy`: test ethical belief movement and expression.
6. `minto-pyramid-principle`: structure the argument.
7. `llm-council`: pressure-test finalists.
8. `copywriting`, `copy-editing`, `prose-editing`, `writing-guidelines`: draft and polish only after approval.
