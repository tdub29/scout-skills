# Independent review, 2026-10-16

Three readers checked all scientific sections against actual PubMed abstracts and selected original fulltexts. All 89 distinct PMIDs cited by the original skill are represented in their source ledgers; 96 distinct substantive records were read including targeted disconfirming and updated evidence. Every original Strong label was reviewed. A separate rotation checks the corrected sections: each reader checks a section they did not initially review. This is a targeted source audit, not an exhaustive systematic review.

## Correction notices

The original inventory had two cited papers with correction links, not merely two correction notices. The 2018 guideline has 2019 and 2023 notices: only parts of the 2019 body were available and the 2023 body was inaccessible. The superseded paper is not used for active clinical guidance.

Hall's 2019 notice (PMID 31269427) was read fully: one subject's meal-sheet diet order changed meal calories/macros; total daily intake and snacks were unaffected. Its separate 2020 notice (PMID 33027677) body remained inaccessible after bounded primary-source attempts. No claim that the second notice spares CRP is made; the CRP inference is withheld.

Fresh NCBI screening found two corrections on the replacement 2026 guideline, which Europe PMC metadata omitted. Actual bodies were read: PMID 42330109 changes an apoB triglyceride qualifier, figure references and a name; PMID 42709919 corrects colesevelam g/mg units and bile-acid-sequestrant dosing frequencies. Neither changes hsCRP. Relevant Appendix 1 industry disclosure rows were read, so this guideline is not called independent tie-free corroboration.
