# Sources for inflammation-evidence

Source inventory reviewed 2026-10-16. Includes retained sources and historical records not relied upon. Original draft retrieval labels were checked against actual abstracts or article bodies; corrections and review limits follow below. **Depth** is what was actually read: "full text" means the open-access article (or, for Pearson 2003, the publisher copy); "abstract" means the PubMed abstract and record only, so a claim from it is limited to what the abstract states. **Screen** is the prescreen result: correction flags; funding; conflict statement; design. "Funding unknown" means no statement was readable, not that the work is clean.

Tiers: **1** = position stand, consensus, guideline or government body. **2** = paper (trial, meta-analysis, cohort, review). **3** = repository and **4** = practitioner sources were not cited; the registry skills screened are listed at the end.

## Independent review and prescreen, 2026-10-16

Three fresh readers reviewed every Strong rule and all scientific sections against actual PubMed abstracts and selected original fulltexts. A source being narrative, observational or statistically pooled was not by itself enough for a Strong grade. Rule-level judgement, population limits and dose/protocol limits are explicit. See `review-2026-10-16.md` for dispositions.

The original 92-record screen passed its known-retraction self-check: 0 retracted, 0 concern, 4 records with erratum links, 7 automated tie flags, 63 with no readable conflict statement from that automated route. Those are screener fields, not a count of truly independent or unfunded papers. The two added records were separately screened: no retraction/concern, sleep source no linked erratum, 2026 guideline linked corrections. Re-run the final inventory screen before installation.

Funding and conflicts are separate. Public grants do not erase author commercial interests or donated products. Sole tied support is downgraded unless an actual matching independent finding or a stated direction-of-bias exception exists. Specific applications: 1.3 industry-body review remains Weak; 2.1/2.2/2.3/2.8 historical mixed-supported guidance is Moderate; 4.3 DII inference is Weak because the sole review has commercial interests; 8.4 exact genetic inference is Weak because its sole source includes GSK employment/shareholding. Vitamin-D marker evidence remains Weak for inconsistent findings and mixed support. EE marker elevation has several distinct corroborating trials; the proteomic paper and its parent RCT are not separate trial replications.

Correction access is not certified where only metadata were obtained. The superseded 2018 guideline is not used for active guidance because its 2019 correction was only partly available and its 2023 body was inaccessible. Hall's 2019 correction was read: one subject's meal-sheet diet order changed meal calories/macros, with total daily intake and snacks unaffected. The distinct 2020 correction body was inaccessible, so the Hall CRP inference is withheld. The current 2026 guideline's separate corrections and actual disclosure rows were read in follow-up; see review record. No inaccessible correction is silently treated as irrelevant.

Known depth corrections: BioCycle 22306563 and diet review 34607347 were verified at abstract/record level; their cached metadata-only files are not called full text. DII 33873204 was subsequently read from a fresh full article with commercial disclosures. Synbiotic 38341803 declares no external work funding plus a government scholarship and no relevant conflicts. VITAL 36558465 had NIH support and donated products; the healthy-young-adult inference is not established. Binge 24828436 and LEA 38936255 were checked in full text; LEA did not increase resting CRP/IL-6/TNF. Stress 28089638 has IL-1beta publication-bias concerns. Registry missing-link counts are not a measured publication-bias correction.
