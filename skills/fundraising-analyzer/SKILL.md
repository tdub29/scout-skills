# Fundraising Analyzer

Frameworks for analyzing nonprofit fundraising performance, segmenting donors, evaluating campaign effectiveness, and building data-driven fundraising strategies.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the question: donor segmentation, campaign performance, health metrics, multi-year trend, or giving-program strategy.
2. Load the matching reference and fill its template with the user's data. Do not invent figures; mark missing inputs as blank.
3. Report results with the comparison basis (prior year, benchmark, or segment) stated.
