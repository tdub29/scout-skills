# Worked Example: A Roster That Should Not Start This Clock

## Worked example: a roster that should NOT start this clock

This is the counter-example, and it is here because the clock was once started on it
by mistake.

State: 8th of 10 in projected starter points, titleOdds_now ~1.4% (0.14x fair share),
Love 21, Achane 24, McBride 26, five firsts, three startable QBs in a 1QB league.

**Do not start the clock. Run `dynasty-roster-phase-diagnosis` first and read the
REACHABLE number.** Here it is 10.2%, or 1.02x fair share, once the picks and the QB
surplus convert. A 7.3x conversion gap means the roster is unconverted, not old and
not weak. Nothing on this roster needs to be torn down to open a window that
converting already opens.

Measured cost of starting the clock anyway, 2500 sims per season:

| Season | Title odds under the teardown | League rank |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 0.0% | 10th |
| 2027 | 0.0% | 10th |
| 2028 | 0.1% | 10th |
| 2029 | 0.4% | 10th |
| 2030 | 1.0% | 10th |

Five consecutive last-place seasons, against a do-nothing baseline that reaches 3rd in
the league by 2029 unaided. Discounted 10-year equity: teardown 21.0%, do nothing
42.5%, convert-and-contend 43.1%. The teardown scored **half of doing nothing**.

**The three preconditions this roster fails.** Start a clock only when all hold:

1. The reachable number is also weak (< 0.6x fair share after conversion). Here it
   is 1.02x.
2. The core cannot reach the flip year in its prime. Here Love arrives at 24 and
   McBride at 29, both fine.
3. There is no cheaper path to the same window. Here doing literally nothing reaches
   3rd by 2029, so the teardown buys a worse version of a free outcome.

**What this roster does instead.** Convert, do not sell. QB2 and QB3 first: ~0.2pp
each to lose, +0.82pp traded for a startable WR. Then the 2027 firsts into producers
aged 24 to 27. Hold Love, hold McBride, hold Achane, hold Evans (selling Evans costs
2.8pp, the largest single loss on the roster).

**When the clock IS right.** A roster whose reachable number is still under 0.6x after
every conversion, whose core cannot arrive in prime, and which holds no cheaper path.
Then: sell seasons 1 and 2, flip before season 3, written triggers dated in the roster
notes before the first trade sends, year-1 gate check on top-8 average age (<= 24.5)
and value share, and termination at the end of season 3 regardless, because a mediocre
flip beats starting over.
