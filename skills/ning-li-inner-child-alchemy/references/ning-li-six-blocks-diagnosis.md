## The framework

### Core thesis

Six recurring **mental block** patterns from childhood caregiver dynamics. A memory may carry **more than one** block â€” note all present; reparent each.

| Block | Origin (childhood) | Adult replay (typical) | Undo direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pressure** | Forced to be/achieve X | Perfectionism, shame when "not enough" in that domain | Release must-do/be; reasonable is okay; parent's happiness â‰  child's job |
| **Attack** | Abuse, harsh punishment, violence | Fear, rage, hypervigilance, self-harm thoughts | Protect child in memory; remove attacker |
| **Neglect** | Emotional/physical absence | Self-neglect, unmet needs, loneliness | Attend; give what was missing |
| **Narcissism** | Parentified caretaker role | Over-function for others; ignore own needs | Not responsible for others' needs; adult self cares for child |
| **Abandonment** | Rejection, leaving, coldness | Clinginess, fear of alone, attachment anxiety | Present, validate, love in memory |
| **Stifle** | Forbidden emotions/traits | Repression, blow-ups in "safe" places, flat affect | Validate stifled part; permission to feel/be |

**Pressure nuance:** Parent pressure on achievement, chastity, sports, sibling care, body size, comparison to others' kids.

**Attack:** Includes physical, emotional, sexual abuse â€” escalate severe ongoing abuse to professionals.

**Narcissism block:** Li's term for child forced to meet parent/sibling needs â€” not clinical narcissism.

1. **Restate memory** in 2â€“3 sentences (user's words).

2. **Score each block** Present / Absent / Unclear with one evidence line from memory.

4. **List adult replay patterns** in today's trigger domain (from trigger map if available).

5. **Output block diagnosis table** + undo direction bullets for primary (and secondary if any).

## Common failure modes

- **Single-block forcing.** Real memories often blend (pressure + stifle common).

- **Mislabeling narcissism block as "narcissist."** Clarify parentification/caretaker dynamic.

- **Skipping attack escalation.** Physical/sexual abuse content requires professional support offer.

- **Typology without memory.** Need linked memory or concrete scene â€” don't diagnose from traits alone.
