## The framework

### Core thesis

Reprogram the memory by entering it as adult self, meeting inner child **in the scene**, and supplying what caregivers failed to supply. Four sub-steps:

1. **Validate** child thoughts/feelings in memory (specific empathy)
2. **Boundary** â€” parent's behavior came from their scripts; child doesn't have to copy
3. **Undo trauma** â€” block-specific rewrite (see table)
4. **Talk to parent** in memory â€” name harm, declare new path; hug child, lead away

After: memory may remain but should lose grip as automatic script.

### Sub-step 1 â€” Validate (example shape)

> "[Child name], I know when [situation], you think [thought] and feel [emotion]. It makes sense you think and feel that way. It's okay."

### Sub-step 2 â€” Boundary

Parent's pain/triggers belong to **them**. Child doesn't have to be who parents wanted or do what they demanded. Mental separation from parent's influence in the memory.

Key line for pressure blocks: Even perfect behavior wouldn't fix parent's unhappiness â€” their pain is theirs.

### Sub-step 3 â€” Undo trauma (by block)

| Block | Undo action |
|---|---|
| **Pressure** | Child doesn't have to do/be X; reasonable is okay; release perfectionism |
| **Attack** | Step between child and attacker; protect; remove attacker from scene |
| **Neglect** | Be with lonely child; give missing attention/care |
| **Narcissism** | Child not responsible for others' needs; you (adult) care for child's needs now |
| **Abandonment** | Be present; support, accept, validate, love |
| **Stifle** | Stifled emotion/trait is acceptable, lovable, human; no need to push down |

Creative undo OK (e.g., unlock bathroom door, walk child out).

### Sub-step 4 â€” Confront parent & exit

Stand between child and parent. Tell parent what they did wrong and how it felt. Declare things will be different. Hug child, hold hand, lead away.

## Common failure modes

- **Skipping validation rush to fix.** Child must feel seen before undo.

- **Undo without boundary.** Child still obeys internalized parent unless separated.

- **Attack undo without safety plan.** Severe abuse â€” professional support, not DIY-only.

- **One-shot expectation.** Li uses many releases over time; single session is one memory.

- **Agent as therapist.** Stay in script/prompt lane; no diagnosing or medication advice.
