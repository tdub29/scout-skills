### Useful questions

- How are you feeling right now?
- Is anything bothering you?
- When was the first time you felt that way?

### DON'Ts

- Shame, blame, or tell child feelings/actions are wrong
- Command STOP feeling or doing
- Ignore/suppress (causes repression explosions)
- "Should / shouldn't" directives

### DO's

- Listen; validate ("It makes sense you think and feel this way")
- Promise protection if afraid
- Say: good not bad; I love you; I forgive you; I'm sorry; I accept you
- Hug
