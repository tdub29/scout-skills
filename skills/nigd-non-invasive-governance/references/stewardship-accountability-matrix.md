
# NIGD Stewardship Accountability Matrix

Use this when governance needs named accountability that fits existing work. The output is a stewardship accountability matrix.

## Artifact

Produce a matrix with:

1. **Data domain or subject area** - the data being governed and why it matters.
2. **Operational stewardship** - people closest to creating, defining, using, or correcting the data.
3. **Tactical stewardship** - domain stewards or coordinators who facilitate cross-area alignment.
4. **Strategic and executive roles** - council members and sponsors who resolve policy, priority, and funding decisions.
5. **Supporting cast** - IT, governance team, metadata, quality, privacy, security, and project roles.
6. **Accountability actions** - decisions each role makes, when they are involved, and how escalation works.

## Process

1. Treat stewardship as a relationship to data, not a job title. Start with who already defines, produces, uses, fixes, or is impacted by the data.
2. Formalize existing accountability before adding new structure. Preserve day jobs while making decision rights visible.
3. Separate operational, tactical, strategic, and support layers. Do not ask one person or council to handle every level of governance work.
4. Define involvement triggers. Specify when a steward is consulted, approves, resolves, communicates, or executes.
5. Make training accountability-focused. Teach people how governance formalizes their existing responsibilities and decision paths.

## Failure Modes

- **Title-first governance** - appointing "data stewards" as a new role without mapping their real relationship to data.
- **Volunteer ambiguity** - asking people to help with governance but not defining decisions, time expectations, escalation, or authority.
- **Council overload** - pushing operational data issues to executives instead of using tactical and operational layers.
- **IT ownership default** - assigning business data accountability to IT because systems store the data.
- **Invasive rollout** - creating a parallel bureaucracy that disrupts how work already gets done.

## Output Format

Return the matrix, unresolved accountability gaps, escalation paths, and the smallest non-invasive next step to formalize ownership.
