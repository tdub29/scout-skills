
# NIGD Activity Matrix

Use this when governance needs to be embedded in work already happening. The output is an activity matrix for proactive and reactive governance involvement.

## Artifact

Produce an Activity Matrix with:

1. **Governed activities** - projects, data definition, issue resolution, quality remediation, access review, metadata updates, policy changes, and lifecycle events.
2. **Activity type** - proactive, reactive, operational, tactical, strategic, or support.
3. **Steward involvement** - who is informed, consulted, accountable, approving, executing, or escalating.
4. **Timing trigger** - when governance enters the activity and what signal starts it.
5. **Output evidence** - decision record, definition, policy, issue ticket, quality rule, approval, or communication.

## Process

1. Avoid calling data governance a standalone process. Instead, identify business and data management processes that need governance participation.
2. Split proactive and reactive activities. Proactive governance prevents issues in projects and changes; reactive governance resolves incidents, conflicts, and quality problems.
3. Define when to involve each role. Involvement too early creates noise; involvement too late creates rework.
4. Attach concrete artifacts to activities. Every governed activity should leave a decision, definition, rule, approval, or documented resolution.
5. Publish the matrix where teams work. The matrix should guide project teams and operational teams, not live only in governance decks.

## Failure Modes

- **Governance process theater** - inventing meetings and workflows that do not connect to actual business processes.
- **Late-stage review** - stewards are asked to approve after requirements, data definitions, or access patterns are already locked.
- **Everywhere involvement** - governance is invited to everything, causing fatigue and avoidance.
- **Reactive-only model** - the team fixes data fires but never inserts governance into projects or change processes that create them.
- **No artifact trail** - decisions happen in meetings but definitions, rules, and accountability records are not updated.

## Output Format

Return the activity matrix, timing triggers, missing artifacts, and a short adoption plan for embedding the matrix into existing workflows.
