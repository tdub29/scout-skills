---

Write concise enterprise copy targeted to Power BI lead and dev manager personas.

Define each output block with:
- Problem statement
- Outcome statement
- Proof statement
- CTA statement

Use deterministic facts from generated artifacts only.
Do not fabricate customer logos, certifications, or benchmark numbers.

When writing landing content:
1. Lead with operational pain and handoff risk.
2. Show deterministic dual-audience outcome.
3. Anchor proof with source references.
4. Close with one primary CTA and one secondary CTA.

Use references/product-messaging.md for reusable copy patterns.
