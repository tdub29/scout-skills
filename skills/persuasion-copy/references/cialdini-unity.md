### Two modes of Unity

**Mode 1: Being Together**

**Mode 2: Acting Together**

3. **Co-creation.** People value items they helped create (IKEA effect). Framing advice-seeking as "I'd value YOUR input" (joint creation) outperforms "here's my opinion" (one-way).

## How to apply

When seeking deeper influence:

1. **Map shared identities.** Family, place, profession, alma mater, fandom, generation. Anything categorical you both belong to.

2. **For Being Together: surface a shared identity BEFORE the request.** Genuine. Not "we're both humans" (too vague). "We both worked at X" or "both alums of Y" or "fellow [community]."

3. **For Acting Together: design joint experience.** Synchronized activity (music, breakwork, walking), reciprocal disclosure (deeper questions in turn), co-creation (asking for advice/input on the substance).

4. **Frame collective: "we" not "you and me."** Language matters. "We're figuring this out together" creates the geography for Unity to operate.

When detecting Unity tactics being used on you:

1. **Notice the "we" framing.** Is someone surfacing a shared identity before asking for something?
2. **Notice synchronized or joint-action invitations.** Group activities before a sales pitch. Mirroring in a sales conversation.
3. **Notice when a request feels weirdly hard to refuse.** Identity-merging makes refusal feel like betrayal-of-self. That's the Unity engine.

## Common failure modes

- **Forced or fake unity.** "We're all family here" said by a CEO to underpaid workers. The framing is too thin; everyone notices.
- **Unity used to extract sacrifice.** Heavy Unity activation followed by exploitative requests crosses into cultic territory.
- **Surface-only Unity.** Mentioning a shared identity without any substance behind it. Unity needs to feel real to operate.
- **Over-claiming.** "We" when there's no actual we. Or unity-words sprinkled over a clearly transactional setup.
- **Ignoring Acting Together when you have time.** Joint experience produces Unity that Being Together can't, fast.

## Ethical guardrail

Unity is among the most powerful influence levers in the catalog â€” and among the most weaponized historically (cults, exploitative groups, political mass-movements). Three sharp checks:

- **Real shared identity, not manufactured fictional one.** Activating actual shared kinship/place/profession is fair; manufacturing an artificial "in-group" to extract compliance is manipulation.
- **No sacrifices that wouldn't be made without the Unity framing.** Genuine kin make sacrifices for kin; if your Unity-tactics extract sacrifices the target wouldn't make outside the manufactured frame, you've crossed a line.
- **Reversibility test.** If the target saw the Unity activation being used strategically, would they endorse it? Genuine community framing â€” usually yes. Strategic manipulation â€” almost always no.
