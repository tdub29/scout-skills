## Need-to-decide (do we even decide?)

- **Q1** â€” Why are we deciding anything at all? Does a decision actually need to be made here?
- **Q4** â€” Why hasn't this decision been made already? Why didn't we decide before?
- **Q5** â€” What's taking so long to make this decision? Why are we hesitating? What does that reveal?
- **Q10** â€” What would happen if we just didn't make the decision?
- **Q22** â€” When do we have to decide?
- **Q31** â€” Do you even care which way this goes? If not, why are you involved?

## Who decides

- **Q2** â€” Is the right person making this decision? Not the right role, but the right person with the right information, context, and insight? Who's merely chiming in?
- **Q21** â€” Could this decision be a good one for someone else to practice making?
- **Q27** â€” Would another opinion help or hinder?
- **Q35** â€” Are we asking multiple people to make a decision that one person should be making?

## Reversibility & size

- **Q7** â€” Can we make this decision smaller? Can we take one big decision and turn it into three smaller ones?
- **Q8** â€” How easily can we reverse the decision?
- **Q14** â€” Is there even a wrong decision?
- **Q23** â€” Will this be a one-and-done decision, or will this be a repeating decision?

## First instinct

- **Q9** â€” What was our first instinct on this decision? Are we now just walking around in circles trying to justify that gut reaction with data?
- **Q28** â€” If we were forced to make a decision right this second, what would it be?

## Cost & payoff

- **Q13** â€” How can we make this decision easier? What parts can we eliminate from consideration?
- **Q20** â€” Will this decision make more work for people that don't have extra time for that work? Or will it eliminate work?
- **Q36** â€” Is the return on effort worth it?
- **Q37** â€” What gets easier if we make this decision? What gets harder? Will easier remain easier in the long term, or is it short-term easy but long-term hard? And vice versa.
- **Q38** â€” In the end, is this about money?

## Context & impact

- **Q6** â€” Why would someone else make a different decision? What's the other side â€” or two or three â€” look like?
- **Q17** â€” What other decisions will be impacted by this decision?
- **Q18** â€” Will this decision eliminate the need to make other decisions, or will it create the necessity to make even more decisions?
- **Q24** â€” Is anyone outside the company depending on this, or is this a decision of our own making?
- **Q25** â€” How does this decision impact customers vs. impact us?
- **Q29** â€” Where do we think we'd be today if we made this decision 90 days ago?
- **Q30** â€” Is there anything in this decision we'd regret if we didn't take X, Y, or Z into consideration?

## Signal & confidence

- **Q11** â€” What happened the last time we made a decision like this?
- **Q19** â€” What missing information would lead to making a different decision?
- **Q26** â€” Is this primarily a data-based decision, or an intuition, gut-based decision?
- **Q32** â€” When and how will we know whether the decision was the right one, or if it even mattered?
- **Q33** â€” When the consequences of our decision appear, are they likely to be visible with the naked eye or do they require a microscope to detect? If the latter, does it even matter?

## Principles

- **Q34** â€” What principles are we bending if we make this decision?

## Time test

- **Q3** â€” If we remove the immediate impact, how do we think we'll feel about this decision a year from now?
- **Q15** â€” Do we anticipate making a different decision if we wait until tomorrow morning to make it?
- **Q16** â€” Is any decision better than no decision, or is no decision better than any decision?

## Emotional check

- **Q12** â€” What are we looking forward to after the decision is made? What are we afraid of?
